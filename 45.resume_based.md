Option A: PO → SO Automation (recommend if they want business impact)

Situation: Sales teams were manually matching incoming Purchase Orders against catalog/pricing data to raise Sales Orders — slow and error-prone at volume.

My contribution: I built the service end-to-end — ingestion of incoming POs, a matching engine that validates and scores line items against catalog/pricing data, and a confidence-scoring layer. Orders above a tuned threshold auto-promote to Sales Orders; below-threshold ones route to a human for review.

Tech stack: Python, FastAPI (API layer), Celery (async matching/scoring jobs), PostgreSQL (catalog/pricing/order data), Redis (job state/caching), Docker + Azure (deployment).

Key design decisions to mention:
- Why a confidence-score threshold instead of binary match/no-match — lets you tune precision vs. automation rate without redeploying.
- Celery for async processing so PO ingestion doesn't block on matching latency.
